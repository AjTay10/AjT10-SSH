# Script skeletons: all 20 starter-kit concepts

Drafts, not scripts. Every number is a `[TEST: …]` placeholder that gets
filled from **your** recorded gameplay before the line is spoken — if a test
placeholder is still empty at edit time, the claim is cut, not guessed
(voice rule 5). Structure follows `hook-craft`: verdict first, no greeting,
steepest-retention-risk zone (0:00–0:30) always delivers the title's promise.

## The skeleton grammar (applies to all 20)

```
COLD OPEN  0:00–0:15   The verdict or the payoff fragment. Screen shows proof
                       footage immediately — never your face, never a logo.
STAKES     0:15–0:40   Why this matters in money/hours. One sentence of method
                       ("I tested this on [date/patch], timer on screen").
BODY       0:40–…      The test or the ranked list. Chapters at every beat.
RECEIPTS   last 60s    The numbers table on screen. Repeat the verdict.
CTA        last 15s    One line, one ask: the next video in the series.
                       Never "like and subscribe" as a sentence.
SHORTS     —           The 30–60s vertical cut marked in each skeleton.
```

Recording session defaults: radio OFF, separate audio tracks, note the patch
version on screen in the first 30 seconds of every money video.

## DAY ONE series (Sat)

### 1. Best First Business in GTA Online (Current Patch)
- **Thumb:** ACID LAB FIRST / BUY · **Length:** 6–8 min
- **Cold open:** "If you have under [TEST: threshold]$ in GTA Online, there
  is exactly one business worth buying first — it's the Acid Lab, and here's
  the math everyone skips." *(Screen: sell-mission payout popping.)*
- **Body beats:** ① What it costs, all-in — [TEST: First Dose completion
  time] + [TEST: equipment upgrade price on current patch]. ② One full solo
  sell, uncut-but-speedran, timer on screen → [TEST: payout, minutes].
  ③ The per-hour math on screen: [TEST: $/hr]. ④ Why not the bunker/nightclub
  *first* — one line each, honest ("great later, not first, because…").
- **Receipts:** cost vs. recoup table — [TEST: number of sells to break even].
- **CTA:** "Day One #2 covers your entire first week — next Saturday."
- **Shorts cut:** the payout moment + per-hour figure, 35s.

### 6. Beginner's First Week — What Actually Matters
- **Thumb:** SKIP THE GRIND / TESTED · **Length:** 8–10 min
- **Cold open:** "Your first week in GTA Online decides whether the game is
  fun or a second job. Do these [N] things, in this order, and skip
  everything else."
- **Body beats:** ordered checklist, each with on-screen proof: ① the free
  claims every new player misses [TEST: verify current freebies], ② First
  Dose → Acid Lab (link video #1), ③ the [TEST] activity that pays best
  pre-businesses, ④ the two purchases that look good and waste your first
  million (name them, show why). ⑤ What NOT to do: bought every failure
  myself so the receipts are real [only claim if true].
- **Receipts:** "end of week one, you should have roughly [TEST: $ range and
  unlocks] — here's my fresh-account proof."
- **Shorts cut:** the free-claims beat, 45s.

### 14. Things I Wish I Knew Before [YOUR REAL HOURS] Hours
- **Thumb:** [N] HOURS LATER / TESTED · **Length:** 8–10 min
- **Cold open:** "[Your real Steam/Social Club hour count on screen.] That
  number bought these [N] lessons. Number one would have saved me
  [TEST-honest estimate framed as estimate] on its own."
- **Body beats:** 7–10 lessons, each ≤60s, each with footage proof, ranked by
  money-or-time saved. At least two must be "everyone recommends X — X is
  wrong, here's my data" (the contrarian slots).
- **Rule:** the hour count is whatever it truly is. 300 real hours beats
  1,000 fake ones — the pinned comment invites viewers to add their lesson.
- **Shorts cut:** lesson #1 alone, 30s.

## TEST BENCH series (Wed)

### 2. Solo Money Guide — No Friends Needed
- **Thumb:** SOLO ONLY / TESTED · **Length:** 10–12 min
- **Cold open:** "Every GTA money guide assumes you have a full crew. I
  tested the top [N] methods completely solo, same session, timer running —
  this is the real ranking."
- **Body beats:** per method (pick 4–5): 30s of method, then the on-screen
  result card [TEST: $/run, min/run, $/hr each]. Rank strictly by tested
  $/hr — if the meta favorite loses, it loses on camera (that clip is the
  channel's best marketing).
- **Receipts:** full ranked table, one screen, 10 seconds of silence to let
  it be screenshot-ed.
- **Shorts cut:** the #1 method's result card, 40s.

### 4. New Account → First $10M, Timed (2–3 part series)
- **Thumb:** $0 → $10M / TESTED · **Length:** 10–12 min per part
- **Cold open:** "Fresh account, zero dollars, no shark cards, no help. The
  clock starts now and stops at ten million. Everything on screen."
- **Body beats:** montage with persistent HUD overlay (session timer +
  bank balance — build the overlay once in your editor). Each milestone gets
  a beat: first business [TEST: at what hour?], first million, the plateau
  and how it broke. End each part on the running total.
- **Rule:** publish the failures too — the deaths and dead-ends make the
  final [TEST: total hours] credible.
- **Shorts cut per part:** milestone moments, 30s each.

### 7. Cayo Perico: Still Worth It? Tested.
- **Thumb:** STILL #1? / TESTED · **Length:** 8–10 min
- **Cold open:** "Cayo Perico was the undisputed money king for years. I ran
  it [N≥5] times on the current patch, stopwatch running — here's whether
  that's still true."
- **Body beats:** ① method: what was timed (setup + finale + cooldown),
  ② the runs, compressed, times visible, ③ [TEST: average $/hr across runs
  — average, not best], ④ head-to-head vs. your Acid Lab and #1 solo-method
  numbers from videos 1–2 (internal suggested-video loop), ⑤ verdict stamp.
- **Shorts cut:** the comparison table reveal, 45s.

### 8. Nightclub: The Setup Most Players Get Wrong
- **Thumb:** ONE WRONG TOGGLE / SKIP? · **Length:** 6–8 min
- **Cold open:** "There's a settings mistake inside the nightclub that
  quietly cuts its income. Check yours against this screen right now."
- **Body beats:** ① the wrong config, shown [TEST: identify the actual
  common misconfiguration on current patch — technician assignment order],
  ② side-by-side: [TEST: income/hr wrong config vs. right config, measured
  over identical sessions], ③ full optimal setup walkthrough, ④ when the
  nightclub is worth buying at all (link video #1).
- **Shorts cut:** "check this toggle" beat, 30s — highest share-bait in the kit.

### 13. Best Armored Vehicle for the Money, Tested
- **Thumb:** TESTED ALL / BUY · **Length:** 8–10 min
- **Cold open:** "I took every armored contender against the same three
  attacks [homing missile / explosive rounds / ram]. Most of them failed.
  One didn't — and it isn't the expensive one." *(Only if your tests show
  that; otherwise say what they do show.)*
- **Body beats:** fixed test protocol stated up front, then per vehicle:
  [TEST: survives N missiles / result per attack / price]. Rank by
  survivability-per-dollar. Show the methodology's limits honestly.
- **Shorts cut:** the winner surviving the missile test, 30s.

## WORTH IT series (Thu)

### 3. What To Buy This Week (the weekly template — reuse forever)
- **Thumb:** WORTH IT · THU chip + week's headline item / BUY or SKIP
- **Length:** 4–6 min. Same structure every week — the template IS the show:
- **Cold open:** "This week's update, ranked: [N] things worth your money,
  [M] things that aren't. Number one: [best item], because [tested reason]."
- **Body beats:** ① BUY list, each with the math on screen ([TEST: discount
  price vs. earn-back time at your tested $/hr rates]), ② SKIP list, each
  one sentence of why, ③ the week's bonus events: which are worth the hours
  [TEST: quick-check payouts], ④ "if you only have $[X]" — one pick per
  budget tier.
- **Positioning:** never race the news channels — publish Thursday evening
  as the *second opinion with receipts* (teardown: TGG owns the morning).
- **Shorts cut:** the ranked list as a rapid vertical, 45s, every week.

### 5. Businesses That Are a Waste of Money
- **Thumb:** DON'T BUY / SKIP · **Length:** 8–10 min
- **Cold open:** "These purchases look like money-makers. I own [only if
  true — otherwise 'I tested'] all of them, and [N] of them will cost you
  more hours than they'll ever pay back."
- **Body beats:** per offender: price, [TEST: measured $/hr or the math on
  its payback period], what to buy instead at the same price point (link
  video #1/#2). One "surprise acquittal" — a hated business your numbers
  defend — for the comment section.
- **Shorts cut:** the worst offender, 40s.

### 15. New DLC/Update: Everything Worth Doing (And What To Skip)
- **Thumb:** WORTH IT? / TESTED · **Length:** 8–12 min · **Timing:** within
  72h of any major (not weekly) update — speed matters here, polish less
- **Cold open:** "The new update is out. I've run everything. Here's the
  order that pays: [top item] first, and skip [flop] entirely."
- **Body beats:** new content ranked by [TEST: payout/fun-per-hour, tested
  same-day]; what changed in the old meta ([TEST: re-run your standard
  $/hr benchmarks — your back catalog becomes the comparison set no
  competitor has]); updated "what to buy first" verdict.
- **Shorts cut:** "the one thing to do first," 30s, publish same day.

### 16. Weekly Update in 90 Seconds (Shorts-first companion to #3)
- **Format:** 60–90s vertical, published Thursday morning — the fast cut
  that funnels to Thursday evening's full verdict video.
- **Beats:** ① what's new (5s), ② the one BUY (with price), ③ the one SKIP,
  ④ "full ranked list with the math tonight — link in comments."
- This is the only slot where you race for speed; it's a Short, so the cost
  of losing the race is one Short.

## UTILITY / SEARCH lane (as demand appears)

### 9. Best Settings for FPS on a Mid-Range PC
- **Thumb:** FREE FPS / TESTED · **Length:** 6–8 min
- **Cold open:** "Same PC, same scene: [TEST: before FPS] versus [TEST:
  after FPS]. Five settings did that. Here they all are, benchmarked."
- **Body beats:** your real spec on screen; per setting: what it does in one
  sentence + [TEST: its isolated FPS delta on your rig]; the final combined
  before/after; "your rig will differ — these are my measurements, run
  yours" (honesty = the brand).
- **Shorts cut:** before/after split-screen, 30s.

### 10. How To Fix [Current Error/Crash]  *(make within 48h of any breakage)*
- **Thumb:** FIXED / TESTED · **Length:** 2–4 min — utility videos are short
- **Cold open:** "Getting [exact error text on screen]? Two minutes, here's
  the fix that worked for me, plus the two that didn't."
- **Body beats:** the fix, click-by-click, tested on your own machine;
  the popular non-fixes and [TEST: whether they did anything]; pinned
  comment maintained as the live status thread.
- **Watch:** the game's subreddit after every patch — first good fix video
  wins the whole query.

### 11. Loading Forever? What Actually Helps, Tested
- **Thumb:** LOAD FASTER / TESTED · **Length:** 4–6 min
- **Cold open:** "I timed [N] popular loading 'fixes' on the same PC, same
  session type. [TEST: K] of them did nothing. Here's what moved the number."
- **Body beats:** stopwatch table per remedy [TEST: seconds each]; the
  placebo list called out by name; the honest conclusion even if it's
  "nothing helps much" — that video still wins the search.
- **Shorts cut:** the results table, 30s.

### 12. How To Win Gunfights (Aim Settings + Movement)
- **Thumb:** STOP DYING / TESTED · **Length:** 8–10 min
- **Cold open:** "You're losing fights before you fire — [TEST/verify: the
  default aim-assist or sensitivity setting that handicaps PC players].
  Fix that, then these three habits."
- **Body beats:** settings walkthrough with why; the three movement habits
  with kill-cam-style examples from your sessions (win AND loss examples);
  a practice routine that takes 10 min/day. No "become a god" promises —
  "lose less, immediately" is the honest, deliverable claim.
- **Shorts cut:** one habit, 30s.

## PC REPORT series (monthly → weekly near GTA VI launch)

### 17. What Your GTA Online Grind Is Worth When GTA VI Arrives
- **Thumb:** BEFORE VI / TESTED · **Length:** 6–8 min
- **Cold open:** "GTA VI launches November 19 — consoles only. If you're on
  PC like me, here's what's confirmed to matter from your GTA Online
  account, and what's wasted grind." [VERIFY: transfer/carry-over facts
  against Rockstar's own statements at time of recording — this changes.]
- **Body beats:** confirmed facts (source on screen, Rockstar posts only);
  the unknowns stated as unknowns; the rational strategy under uncertainty
  (what to grind, what to stop); the PC reality check and this channel's
  launch-window plan (audience buy-in moment).
- **Shorts cut:** "consoles only — here's what that means on PC," 45s.

### 18. GTA VI: What We Actually Know (No Rumors)
- **Thumb:** FACTS ONLY / TESTED · **Length:** 6–8 min · refresh monthly
- **Cold open:** "Everything in this video has a Rockstar source on screen.
  If it's a leak, it's not here. Here's the full confirmed list in
  [N] minutes."
- **Body beats:** date/platforms/editions with sources displayed; what the
  trailers actually show vs. what commentary claims they show; the "commonly
  repeated, never confirmed" list — naming the rumor economy without joining
  it. This video is the channel's credibility flag; it must be flawless on
  sourcing.
- **Shorts cut:** the confirmed-list rapid-fire, 45s.

## Production notes that apply to all 20

- **The result-card overlay** (method / $ / minutes / $-per-hour) is your
  most reusable edit asset. Build it once in DaVinci as a template; it's
  also your visual watermark against content thieves.
- **Internal linking is the growth loop:** every Test Bench video cites the
  others' numbers; every Worth It verdict links the test that produced its
  math. Suggested-video traffic between your own uploads is how small
  channels compound (`growth-loops`).
- **AI-assist disclosure:** these skeletons are planning docs — your
  recorded gameplay, your voice, your verified numbers make the videos
  yours. No synthetic-media disclosure is needed for that. If you ever use
  an AI voice or generated visuals instead, toggle YouTube's synthetic
  content disclosure and expect it to hurt (see the plan's automation
  assessment).
- **If a [TEST] fails** — the method got patched, the number came out
  boring — that's a video too, often a better one: "everyone says $X/hr;
  I measured [TEST]." The brand is the measuring, not the hype.
