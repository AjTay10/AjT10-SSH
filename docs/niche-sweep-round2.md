# Niche sweep round 2 — 10 niches vs. a ≥15/20 bar, Etsy excluded

Run 2026-08-20 at the user's direction: keep budget-by-paycheck spreadsheets
as #1, test 10 new niches against a raised bar (≥15 of top 20 with both a
verifiable paid-sales signal and a listing-visible flaw), no Etsy.
Instruments: Envato family (public lifetime sales counts, rating counts,
last-update dates), itch.io (live activity-weighted top-sellers ranking),
Shopify Theme Store (live popularity ranking, review counts).

## Result: zero of ten niches meets ≥15/20 on confirmed counts

| Niche | Confirmed pairs | Checked-item flaw rate | Market state |
|---|---|---|---|
| Photoshop actions/presets | 11/20 | 92% (11/12) | Dead catalog, closed marketplace |
| Stock music (AudioJungle) | 12/20 | 100% (12/12) | Dead catalog, closed marketplace, AI-exposed |
| Fonts (GraphicRiver) | 12/20 | 100% (12/12) | Dead catalog, closed marketplace |
| After Effects templates | 9–11/20 | ~75–90% | Dying; ~2–3 sales/week at the top |
| Print templates | 6/20 (6/6 checked) | 100% | Dead catalog, closed marketplace |
| HTML site templates | 6/20 | 50% | Alive; new Tailwind/AI templates enter weekly chart |
| 2D game art (itch.io) | 5–9/20 | ~33–60% | Hot and live; top shelf clean |
| Shopify themes | 3/20 | ~23% | Healthy, fast-turnover, all-2026 updates |
| Game audio/UI (itch.io) | 3/20 | 25% | Live; thin per-item revenue |
| WordPress plugins | 2/20 | 17–25% | Healthy; incumbents ship 2026 updates + AI features |

Three niches (actions, music, fonts) would extrapolate past 15/20 if all 20
items were checked (their checked flaw rates are 92–100%). All three are
disqualified anyway, twice over:

1. **The marketplace is closed.** Envato stopped accepting new author
   applications in April 2026 and does not plan to reopen theme, code,
   photo, audio, or graphics intake (fonts possibly later in 2026); it also
   moved all sellers to a flat 50% revenue share from July 2026. The flawed
   incumbents cannot be competed with *on the venue where their sales live*.
2. **The demand is archaeological.** Every high-flaw-density top-20 is a
   lifetime-sales leaderboard of items created 2010–2019; weekly velocity
   where measurable is 2–3 sales at the top; zero 2024–2026 items rank.

## The structural law this round proved

**Flaw density is inversely proportional to market health.** The ≥15/20 bar
is only reachable in markets that are dying — flaws accumulate precisely
because incumbents no longer need to maintain (closed marketplace, departing
demand). In every living market measured (Shopify themes, WordPress
plugins, itch.io game assets), competition keeps the top shelf clean:
2026-dated updates, 91–100% ratings, multi-format delivery, active comment
response. A high-flaw-density top-20 in a live market does not exist in
this dataset — 23 niches now examined across both rounds.

Implication: the 15/20 criterion selects for graveyards. The correct
criterion remains the original one — moderate flaw density (5+/20) **plus
current sales velocity** — which exactly one niche has met:
budget-by-paycheck spreadsheets (8/18, 37–167 sales/week directional).
It stays ranked #1, unchallenged by this round.

## Actionable residue (below-bar but real, live-market edges)

- **HTML templates**: the one Envato niche where new entrants demonstrably
  break in — Tailwind/AI-startup templates taking multiple weekly-chart
  slots (~8–15 sales/wk) against legacy incumbents. Venue risk: author
  intake closed; entering means selling from one's own site or alternative
  marketplaces.
- **itch.io game audio**: open marketplace, live demand (a 5-day-old pack
  hit #1), and a repeatable flaw pattern in incumbents (no loops, single
  format, unanswered buyers). Thin per-item revenue; an edge, not a lane.
- **itch.io serialized art packs**: completeness/documentation drift in
  popular packs (missing promised assets, docs locked in Discord) — a
  quality wedge for a maintainer-type seller.

## Standing recommendation

Unchanged: run gate 2 on budget-by-paycheck spreadsheets (one product,
≤20 hours, <3 stranger sales in 60 days → stop). No niche found in either
round beats it on the only combination that matters — verified flawed
incumbents in a market that is still buying.
