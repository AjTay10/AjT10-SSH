# $200/month "completely passive" income — the honest audit and the plan

Requested: the best, proven, easiest-to-scale, completely passive way to make
$200/month from home. Evaluated as a skeptical buyer, using the repo's own
discipline: base rates first, falsification before building, no fabricated
numbers.

## 1. The claim, decomposed

"Completely passive" + "from home, no stated capital" + "easiest to scale" is
a three-way contradiction. Every offer sold under all three labels at once is
a course-seller's pitch, and the person selling it earns their income
*actively*, by selling it to you. You can have any one of the three cheaply.
You can have two with either capital or labor. Nobody verifiable has all
three.

## 2. The only business that actually passes the test

**Yield on capital.** It is the single proven, genuinely passive,
perfectly linearly scaling income stream that exists.

- Target: $200/month = $2,400/year
- Top no-strings high-yield savings APY, verified 2026-08-20: **~4.15%**
  → requires **≈ $57,800** in capital
- 3-month T-bill yield, verified 2026-08-20: **3.79%**
  → requires **≈ $63,300** (state-tax-exempt interest narrows the gap)

Zero hours/month. Scales by adding dollars, not effort. FDIC-insured or
Treasury-backed. This is what "completely passive" costs: roughly **$58–63k
of capital per $200/month**, at rates that are currently drifting *down*
(Fed target 3.50–3.75%, unchanged five announcements running).

If you do not have the capital, then by definition you are not buying passive
income — you are starting a business, and a business is work. Everything
below is honest about that.

## 3. Base rates on the popular "passive income" businesses

```
Reference class:  solo operators starting a "passive income" side business
                  from a standing start, first 12 months
```

| Model | Honest read (estimates flagged as such) |
|---|---|
| Dropshipping | Paid-traffic arbitrage against thin margins; active daily ops (ads, support, refunds). Not passive at any point. |
| YouTube "automation" | Success requires audience outcomes that are distribution lottery. Per this repo's base-rates skill: a new channel reaching self-sustaining audience in 12 months is **low single-digit %**, and "go viral" as a plan rounds to zero. |
| Amazon KDP / low-content books | Marketplace is saturated post-AI; median title earns near nothing. I could not find a reliable current median-earnings figure — treat every "KDP income report" as survivorship marketing. |
| Vending machines / rental arbitrage | Real income, but it is capital **plus** logistics labor. Semi-passive at best; not from-home. |
| Niche/affiliate SEO sites | 12–18 month payback historically; AI answer engines are actively compressing the click-through this model lives on. Rising-risk, not proven-stable. |
| Digital products (templates, spreadsheets, printables) on a search-driven marketplace | The best of a weak field: front-loaded build, marketplace brings the demand, maintenance is low. Most sellers still earn ~nothing — the median is dismal, and I will not invent a precise figure — but the *cost of finding out* is the lowest on this list. |

Adjusters that would legitimately move these odds: you have already sold this
kind of product before, or you already own distribution (an audience, an
email list). Enthusiasm, effort, and "my idea is better" move nothing.

## 4. The plan (the last row, done with kill criteria)

Semi-passive digital product on a search-driven marketplace (Etsy digital
downloads / Gumroad / Creative Market class). Chosen not because it is
likely — it isn't — but because it is the cheapest of these bets to
falsify, needs no capital, no inventory, no fulfillment, and a validated
product needs only hours per month after launch.

**Do not build first. Run the kill-test first:**

```
Load-bearing belief:  Buyers are already searching a specific marketplace for
                      a specific product type, and the current top results
                      are beatable on quality or specificity.
Falsifiable form:     Within 7 days of research (≤ 8 hours total), identify a
                      niche where ≥ 5 of the top 20 marketplace search results
                      show recent-sale signals AND have a visible, specific
                      flaw you can name (outdated, ugly, generic, missing the
                      obvious variant).
Cheapest test:        Marketplace search + review mining. Cost: hours, $0.
Kill threshold:       Can't find such a niche in 8 hours → stop. Do not
                      "broaden the search". The market is telling you the
                      shelf is full.
Second gate:          Ship ONE minimum product into that niche (≤ 20 hours
                      build). If < 3 sales to strangers in 60 days at a
                      non-trivial price, stop. Strangers' money is the only
                      evidence that counts; friends' purchases are noise.
What a pass earns:    Permission to build products 2–5 in the same niche —
                      not permission to quit anything or buy any course.
```

**Timeline honesty:** if both gates pass, $200/month steady-state is
realistically a 6–12 month outcome across a small catalog of products, with
2–4 hours/month of maintenance (marketplace changes, customer questions,
refreshes). That is "mostly passive after front-loaded work" — the honest
maximum for a no-capital start.

**Plan for the 90% case:** the modal outcome of this plan is a handful of
sales and a dead storefront. The 90%-case cost is capped by the gates above
at roughly 30 hours and $0–50 — which is exactly the point. If the 90% case
instead looked like "six months and $3,000 gone", the plan would be wrong.

## 5. The contrarian summary

- The proven passive income business is **being a lender**: ~$58–63k of
  capital at today's rates buys $200/month forever, from your couch.
- Every no-capital alternative is a **front-loaded labor business with a
  decaying annuity attached** — legitimate, but sold to you under a false
  label.
- The most profitable move in the "passive income" economy is *selling* the
  dream, which is why every guru's actual income stream is a course. Do not
  become their customer; if anything, note the asymmetry.
- Anything whose pitch requires the word "completely" fails this repo's own
  test: it is a claim no observation is allowed to falsify.
