**What's verified vs. not.** Subscriber counts and positioning descriptions
below come from a public channel directory and public channel profiles,

non-browser clients, so per-video medians, abandoned formats, and comment

## Five reads from the roster

**1. The weekly-update lane has a king with a structural moat — route around
him, don't race him.** TGG owns Thursday coverage, and his moat is literally
the rotation of the earth: uploading from Australia, his update video is live
while most of the world is still on Wednesday. You cannot out-early that from
a US/EU PC. What his speed format structurally cannot hold is *depth of
verdict* — a ranked buy/skip list with the math on screen takes hours he
doesn't spend because he doesn't need to. Your weekly video is the
*second-opinion* video: "the update dropped this morning; here's what's
actually worth your money, tested." Different search intent, same weekly
demand.

**2. The money-guide midfield is an undifferentiated sea — which is the
positioning gap.** Five channels between 200K and 600K describe themselves
in nearly interchangeable words: money guides, business guides, updates,
vehicle reviews. When incumbents are positioned identically, a new entrant
doesn't need to out-produce them — it needs to be *describable in a sentence
none of them can claim*. None of them says "every number tested on screen."

**3. Broughy1322 is the proof the rigor brand works — and it's unclaimed
outside racing.** 292K subscribers built almost entirely on methodical,
tested car-performance data. The audience for "actually measured, not
hyped" demonstrably exists in this niche at scale. Broughy occupies it for
*racing* only. Nobody on this roster is the Broughy of *money* — verified
per-hour rates, timer on screen, methodology stated. That is the exact
brand the recon's solo-money finding called for, with an existence proof
attached.

**4. Gta Rav1sh (26.8K) is your weight-class model.** The one roster channel
near your 18-month horizon lives on efficiency mechanics — "how this
actually works" explainers. That corroborates the whole plan: at small
scale, the mechanism-explainer/search lane is what sustains a channel, not
personality or news. This is the channel whose recent history to study
first in your manual pass.

**5. Nobody on the roster is PC-specific.** Every channel serves the general
(mostly console) audience. The stranded-PC-player lane for the GTA VI window
(recon Finding 1) has no incumbent here — it is genuinely open.

## OUR MOVE (teardown conclusion)

POSITION       "Every number on this channel was tested on screen."
