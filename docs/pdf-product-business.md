# The PDF digital asset business — researched, chosen, and built

Follow-up to [passive-income-teardown.md](passive-income-teardown.md). That
document argued the digital asset model was the only candidate that survives
scrutiny. This one does the next step: research what top sellers actually
provide, pick the most duplicable model, and build the first product. The
flagship product now exists in this repo:
`products/budget-reset-planner/The-Budget-Reset-Planner.pdf` (14 pages,
generated by `generate_planner.py` — edit the script, rerun, republish).

## 1. What the research found

Sources checked on 2026-08-21 (search results, quality-flagged below):

- Etsy's own market pages for [top-selling digital downloads](https://www.etsy.com/market/top_selling_digital_downloads),
  [top-selling printables](https://www.etsy.com/market/top_selling_printables), and
  [best-selling digital templates](https://www.etsy.com/market/best_selling_digital_templates)
  — the closest thing to ground truth, since they reflect real listings.
- Industry listicles: [Outfy](https://www.outfy.com/blog/top-selling-digital-products-on-etsy/),
  [LitCommerce](https://litcommerce.com/blog/digital-products-to-sell-on-etsy/),
  [Insight Agent](https://www.insightagent.app/trends/digital-trends),
  [Conversion Pro Plus (Gumroad trends)](https://conversionproplus.com/blog/gumroad-trends-2026-what-s-selling-right-now),
  [Amasty](https://amasty.com/blog/best-digital-products-to-sell/),
  [Insight Raider (Gumroad category revenue)](https://insightraider.com/en/answers/what-digital-products-sell-best-on-gumroad).

**Source quality warning, per this repo's own rules:** the listicles are
SEO content, often selling a related service; their specific dollar figures
(price bands, per-product revenue) could not be independently verified and
are treated here as directional only, not as facts. What *is* consistent
across every source, including Etsy's own market pages, is the category
ranking — and category direction is all this plan needs.

**The categories every source agrees top sellers occupy:**

1. **Printable planners and organizers** — budget planners, ADHD/wellness
   planners, habit and fitness trackers. The most-cited category everywhere;
   individual planner listings are reported reaching tens of thousands of
   sales.
2. **Business and social-media templates** (mostly Canva-based) — B2B-ish
   buyers, higher prices.
3. **Wedding suites** — invitations, seating charts, programs. High order
   value, seasonal, design-competitive.
4. **Printable wall art** — highest volume, lowest price, most saturated,
   most exposed to AI image flooding.
5. **Educational printables** — teacher resources, kids' worksheets; repeat
   buyers, modest prices.
6. (Gumroad side) **Notion templates, AI prompt packs, dev/design assets** —
   real, but the top revenue sits in software/dev products that require
   audience or expertise this plan doesn't assume.

**One meta-finding worth more than any listicle:** searching for *honest
seller income* surfaced mostly Gumroad listings selling "1000 digital
product ideas" PDFs. The loudest voices in this market monetize aspirants,
exactly as the teardown predicted. Copy the quiet sellers with 20,000 sales
on a $9 planner, not the loud ones selling maps to the gold.

## 2. The model chosen, and why

**Category: the budget & debt-payoff planner** — the intersection of every
filter that matters:

- **Top-seller evidence** — planners are the #1 or #2 category in every
  source including Etsy's own pages; budget/debt planners are a perennial
  sub-niche within it, not a trend.
- **Legally duplicable** — what top sellers provide is a *format* (monthly
  budget sheet, bill tracker, debt snowball, savings tracker). Formats and
  ideas are not copyrightable; their specific design and text are. Build
  original pages in the proven format and you are copying the *market*,
  not the product.
- **Evergreen demand with a self-renewing trigger** — money stress does not
  go out of season, and every January (and every payday resolution) mints
  new buyers.
- **Undated = build once, sell forever** — no yearly rebuild, no dated
  inventory expiring. This is the strongest passivity claim in the whole
  category.
- **Reproducible without design talent** — it's typography, tables, and
  restraint, which is why it could be built as code
  (`generate_planner.py`) in an afternoon. That script *is* the duplication
  machine: every niche variant is an edit, not a redesign.
- What was rejected: wall art (AI flood, race to $3), weddings (design
  competition, seasonal), prompt packs (fad risk, zero moat).

## 3. What was actually built

`The-Budget-Reset-Planner.pdf` — undated, US Letter, print-at-home, 14
pages: cover, how-to-use, money snapshot, monthly budget (print 12×), bill
payment tracker, expense log (print 12×), debt inventory, debt snowball
plan, color-in payoff tracker, sinking funds, savings goal tracker,
subscription audit, 30-day no-spend challenge, year-in-review. All vector,
prints clean in black-and-white or color. Listing copy ready to paste is in
`products/budget-reset-planner/LISTING.md`.

## 4. The duplication playbook (this is the business, not the PDF)

**The ladder — same buyer, deeper products:**

| Rung | Product | Price band* | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Budget Reset (built) | single-digit $ | done |
| 2 | Niche variants: couples / freelancer irregular-income / college edition | single-digit $ | edit the script |
| 3 | The full bundle: planner + all variants + wall-tracker pack | low-double-digit $ | packaging |
| 4 | Interactive Edition (built): auto-calculating browser app, `interactive/The-Budget-Reset-Interactive.html` — sells alone or bundled as the premium tier | low-double-digit $ | done |

*Price bands deliberately vague: set them by pricing against the top 10
current listings for the same search terms on launch day, not against this
document.

**Distribution, in order:**

1. **Etsy first** — it is the only marketplace on the list with built-in
   search demand for exactly this product. The listing (title + 13 tags) is
   the entire marketing at rung 1.
2. **Every download includes a one-page "get the companion pack" insert**
   pointing at a simple landing page with email capture — the only channel
   no platform can repossess.
3. **Pinterest** (not Instagram) — printables are one of the few niches
   where Pinterest still drives meaningful evergreen traffic; pin the
   planner's page mockups.
4. Gumroad/own-site as the second storefront only after Etsy proves demand.

**Operating cadence once live:** answer messages twice a week, refresh the
listing photos quarterly, ship one new variant a month until the ladder is
full — then the maintenance honestly drops to hours per month.

## 5. Kill criteria and honesty clause

The falsification plan from the teardown applies verbatim: list it, and if
after 60 days of live impressions there are **zero stranger purchases**,
the niche variant is falsified — change the niche served (rung 2 exists for
this reason), not the fonts. Track hours; at day 90 compare dollars-per-hour
against "index the money instead."

What this document does not claim: that this planner will sell, any revenue
figure, or that "passive" means zero work. The research supports the
category, the product is real and original, and the marginal cost of the
next variant is an afternoon. That — not a promised number — is the whole
case.
