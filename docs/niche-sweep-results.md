`kill-test-1-results.md`. Same bar per niche: ≥5 of ~20 top marketplace
results with BOTH a verifiable paid-sales signal AND a nameable,
listing-visible flaw. Instruments: GraphicRiver/CodeCanyon (exact public
lifetime sales counts), Creative Market (review counts + dates), Notion
marketplace, sellers' own storefronts, Etsy via search snippets
(directional — Etsy 403-blocks direct fetches), and EtsyHunt third-party

## The winner, and exactly what it earns

**Budget-by-paycheck / debt-payoff spreadsheets** is the only niche that
passes the pair count AND shows current sales velocity:

- Incumbents with 3,500 / 5,300 / 251 / 103 snippet-reported reviews are
  **Google-Sheets-only** — the flaw appears in the listing titles
  themselves, at scale.
- Third-party tracker estimates put top listings at 16,374 and 13,958
  lifetime sales with 150–167 sales/week *this period* — the only
  recent-velocity numbers obtained anywhere in this sweep.
- Secondary flaws: PLR commodity clones (a 103-review listing openly
  sells resale rights) and year-branded templates that expire.
- The wedge that follows from the flaw pattern: a dual-platform
  (Sheets + Excel, genuinely equivalent), undated, paycheck-period-flexible
  (weekly/biweekly/semi-monthly) budget template — the exact combination
  the flawed majority does not ship.

**Caveats that stay attached to this pass:** every Etsy review count is
snippet-derived (Etsy 403s direct reads) and EtsyHunt figures are a
third-party tracker's estimates, not Etsy-published numbers. Two review
counts had ambiguous listing attribution and were conservatively counted.
This is a directional pass, the strongest in the sweep — not an audited one.

**What the pass earns (unchanged from the original plan):** permission to
run gate 2 only — ship ONE minimum product into this niche (≤20 hours
build), and if it takes fewer than 3 sales to strangers in 60 days at a
non-trivial price, stop. It does not earn a catalog, a course, or a quit.

## Why the "pass on letter" niches (2–4) are not recommended

All their verified sales counters are lifetime totals parked on items
last touched 4–16 years ago, on a marketplace (Envato) where newly
created items in the same top-20 sell zero to one copies. That is
archaeology, not demand. Entering there competes for traffic that has
visibly left.

## The structural finding the sweep kept repeating

The verifiably successful sellers in every passing or near-passing niche
share one property that is not the niche: distribution. The "top 1%"
outcome (Easlo, Thomas Frank, the 12.5k-review Etsy shops) was built on
audiences that predate or accompany the product. Niche selection is the
commodity layer; the defensible layer is owning a channel. Any follow-on
plan should treat the product as the monetization of a channel, not the
other way around.
