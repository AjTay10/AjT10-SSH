# Engagement terms

Send this with the quote, before any work starts. Get a written "agreed" back.

**This is a plain-language template, not legal advice, and it has not been
reviewed by a lawyer.** If engagements get larger or cross borders, have one
look at it. The cost of that review is small against a single dispute.

---

## Engagement note

**Between:** {your name / entity} ("I") and {client} ("you")
**Date:** {date}
**Fee:** {$1,200 / $1,800}, payable {on delivery / 50% up front}
**Turnaround:** {3 / 2} business days from receipt of complete data

### What I will do

Analyze the data you supply and deliver a written report covering:

- Concentration of traffic and revenue, and what the total rests on
- Decay: whether recent output performs like earlier output did
- Trend reality: whether apparent growth or decline survives a changepoint test
- Statistical significance of any improvement you or a counterparty claims
- An explicit list of what the data does **not** establish

### What this is not

The report is a presentation of descriptive statistics computed from data you
supply. It is expressly **not**:

- a valuation, appraisal, or opinion on price
- investment, financial, legal, tax, or accounting advice
- a recommendation to buy, sell, list, or transact on any terms
- an audit, or any verification that the data you supply is accurate,
  complete, or truthful
- a warranty as to any future outcome, price achieved, or transaction result

I do not independently verify your figures. Where the underlying data is
inaccurate or incomplete, the analysis will reflect that and I am not
responsible for conclusions drawn from it.

### Conflicts

I will not act for both sides of the same transaction, at any point, on any
terms. If I am already engaged by a counterparty to your deal I will tell you
immediately and decline.

I have no financial interest in whether your transaction completes, at what
price, or with whom.

### Your data

- Used only to produce your report.
- Not shared with any third party.
- Deleted within 30 days of delivery, or immediately on your written request.
- Please remove personal data (names, email addresses, IP addresses,
  customer records) before sending. I do not need it and will not retain it.
- **Aggregate, non-identifying statistics** — for example "content sites in
  this revenue band show a median effective page count of 18" — may be
  retained for benchmarking. Nothing that identifies you, your business, or
  your figures is ever published or shared. Tell me if you prefer to be
  excluded and you will be, at no cost.

### Publication

I sometimes publish anonymized methodology write-ups. Your business, listing,
URL, niche specifics, and figures are never identifiable in these. I will not
publish anything about your engagement without your written consent.

### Liability

My total liability arising from this engagement is limited to the fee paid.
I am not liable for indirect or consequential loss, including any loss arising
from a transaction you enter into or decline to enter into.

### Cancellation

You may cancel before I begin analysis for a full refund. Once analysis has
begun the fee is payable in full, and you receive whatever has been completed.

### Agreement

Reply "agreed" to this email and it takes effect.

## Notes to self — not part of the client document

- **Never soften the "not a valuation" clause**, even when a client pushes for
  a number. The moment you quote a price you are doing a different job, in a
  different regulatory posture, with different liability.
- The **benchmark retention clause** is load-bearing for the long-term asset.
  Offering opt-out at no cost keeps it fair; almost nobody takes it.
- 50% up front for buyer-side work with a deadline. Full payment on delivery is
  fine for sellers, who are not on a clock.
- Keep the signed reply. An email saying "agreed" is a contract in most
  jurisdictions and is enough for engagements this size.
