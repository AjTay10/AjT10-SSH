# Amazon affiliate operating playbook

Written as of August 2026. The honest version, not the course-seller version.

**The verdict up front:** Amazon Associates is a real but *decaying* income
stream. Amazon cut commissions by up to 50% in several categories in May 2026,
removed same-category "halo" attribution from onsite commissions in April
2026, and has a history of unilateral cuts (April 2020 was worse). You are a
price-taker on a platform that has cut your pay twice in six years.

The contrarian read that follows from this: **never build the business on the
commission — build an audience asset that Amazon happens to monetize first.**
Amazon is the lowest-friction way to turn attention into money (everyone has
an account, everyone trusts the checkout, conversion is unmatched). It is the
*entry-level* monetization layer. The upgrade path — brand-direct Amazon
attribution deals, sponsorships, your own products — only exists if you own
the audience. Every model in this playbook is chosen so that the asset you
build (a video library, a channel, a list of buyers) survives the next rate
cut.

## The math nobody shows you

Planning arithmetic with stated assumptions — replace with your own numbers
in `tracker-template.csv` as soon as you have them:

```
Goal:                $3,000 / month
Blended commission:  3%          (most physical categories are 1–4.5%)
→ Referred revenue:  $100,000 / month
Average order:       $40 (assumption)
→ Orders needed:     2,500 / month
Conversion assumed:  10% of clicks buy something within the cookie window
→ Clicks needed:     25,000 / month  ≈  830 / day, every day
```

830 clicks a day is not a side-hustle blog with 12 posts. It is either (a) a
content library of hundreds of pieces compounding over 12–24 months, or (b)
placement directly on Amazon product pages where the traffic already exists
(the Influencer Program). That's why the model ranking below looks the way
it does.

Also load-bearing: the standard cookie is **24 hours** (90 days only for
items added to cart inside that window). You get paid for purchase intent
you catch *at the moment of decision*, not for awareness.

## Models ranked (2026)

**1. Amazon Influencer Program — onsite shoppable videos.** You film short
(60–90s) honest review videos of products you own; once Amazon approves you
for onsite placement, they appear on the product pages themselves, in front
of buyers at the moment of purchase. You bring no traffic — Amazon does.
This is the highest proven ROI-per-hour model for anyone willing to be on
camera, and it is a pure volume game: the operators who earn from it have
hundreds of videos live. Caveats: entry requires an existing social account
(no official follower minimum; in practice a few thousand engaged followers),
onsite approval requires 3 qualifying sales within 180 days, and the April
2026 change means you now earn only on the exact ASIN (or variant) you
reviewed — the same-category halo is gone, which cut everyone's onsite
earnings. Still first, but thinner than the 2023–2024 gold-rush numbers
you'll see on YouTube.

**2. YouTube search-intent review content.** "Best X for Y", "X vs Y",
"X review 6 months later" — long-tail, boring, high-intent queries. Videos
rank for years, intent is purchase-stage so conversion is strong, and the
channel is an asset Amazon can't cut. Slowest to start, most durable, and it
compounds with model 1 (same footage, two placements).

**3. Niche deal distribution (Telegram / Discord / broadcast channel).**
Price-drop alerts in ONE vertical (not "deals" generally — that's saturated
and owned by giants). Powered by Keepa alerts; see `SOURCING.md`. Works
because urgency + a real discount does the selling. Note: Amazon **prohibits
affiliate links in email** — that's why this runs on chat channels, not a
newsletter. Low commission per unit, high volume, fastest feedback loop of
the three.

**4. Niche website / SEO — support asset only.** The default advice of
2015–2022 ("start a niche site") is no longer proven. Google's helpful
content updates and AI Overviews gutted affiliate content sites; the survivors
do genuine hands-on testing and have brand search demand. Have a site as the
home for your video content and FTC disclosures. Do not make it the primary
bet.

**Avoid entirely:** paid ads direct-linked to Amazon (April 2026: purchases
via paid/boosted ads no longer count as qualifying sales, and the unit
economics never worked at 3% commission); AI-written "best X" listicle sites
(you are the supply glut, not the arbitrage); coupon/toolbar plays; anything
that starts with buying a $997 course.

## The recommended stack

One niche → own the products → film once, publish thrice:

1. **Onsite** — the review video goes on the Amazon product page (model 1).
2. **YouTube** — the long version targets the search query (model 2).
3. **Short-form** — the cut goes to TikTok/Reels/Shorts with the storefront
   link, which also maintains the active-social requirement Amazon checks.

Then the daily sourcing pipeline (`SOURCING.md`) feeds it, the tracker
records what actually earns, and the 90-day plan (`NINETY-DAYS.md`) tells
you when to double down and when to kill it.

## Verify, don't trust

Commission rates, attribution rules, and eligibility have all changed inside
the last 18 months and will change again. Before acting on any number in this
playbook, check the live sources: the **Commission Income Statement** and
**Program Operating Agreement** in Associates Central, and the Influencer
Program help pages. Any figure here is a snapshot dated August 2026.
