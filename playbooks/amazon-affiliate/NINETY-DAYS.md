# The 90-day execution plan

Base rate, stated plainly: most people who start affiliate marketing earn
approximately nothing, because most people publish a handful of pieces,
see silence, and quit. The plan below is designed around the two real
failure modes — quitting before the compounding starts, and grinding for a
year on a niche that was dead on arrival. Volume answers the first;
tripwires and kill criteria answer the second.

## Days 1–7 — setup week

- Niche chosen through the gate in `SETUP.md` §1. In writing, with the
  scores. (Use the `decision-log` skill: alternatives, confidence, and the
  kill criteria below attached.)
- Social account active (or a 4–8 week pre-season started if from zero).
- Associates application in. Storefront application in.
- Tracker copied, Keepa alerts set, sourcing bookmarks made.
- First batch filmed: 5 products you already own.

## Days 8–30 — volume and the first 3 sales

- **Publish daily or near-daily.** Target: 25–30 pieces live by day 30.
  At this stage output volume is the only metric that matters; polish is
  procrastination wearing a work costume.
- Run the daily sourcing SOP every working day.
- **Explicit milestone: 3 qualifying sales.** Your Associates account dies
  at day 180 without them, and onsite review can't start. Route: short-form
  with the storefront link + one or two genuinely useful "best X" answers
  posted where your niche already asks (Reddit answers with disclosure,
  YouTube). Not allowed: your own purchases, purchases made on your behalf,
  anything via paid ads.
- Do NOT check earnings daily. Log weekly. Daily checking of a 24-hour-cookie
  business produces noise, despair, and strategy thrash.

## Days 31–60 — feedback and the onsite unlock

- 3 sales in hand → **request the Influencer onsite placement review.**
  Submit your three best-performing honest reviews, not your three favorites.
- Read the tracker: which products, angles, and price points got clicks?
  Make the next 30 pieces 70% "more like what worked", 30% new bets.
- Start the long-form YouTube versions of your best short reviews —
  search-intent titles pulled from the autocomplete phrasings you logged.
- Weekly: `anomaly.py` on clicks; monthly: `concentration.py` on earnings.

## Days 61–90 — double down or kill

- If onsite is approved: shift the mix hard toward onsite volume — film
  every qualifying product you own, then let winners tell you what to buy
  next (only reinvest commissions, never fresh capital, into inventory).
- If a channel is compounding (impressions growing month over month):
  double its cadence, drop the flattest channel.
- Day 90: full tracker read + the `attack` command on your own plan. Decide
  scale-up / pivot / kill with data, not narrative.

## Tripwires (act, don't emote)

| Signal | By | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Associates application declined | week 1 | Fix the surface (site/channel quality), reapply; it's a content-quality signal, not a verdict |
| < 3 qualifying sales | day 60 | Diagnose which stage is dead — no views (hook/format problem), views but no clicks (wrong products or buried links), clicks but no orders (wrong audience intent). Fix that stage only |
| < 100 total outbound clicks/month | day 45 | Channel or format problem. Change format, not effort |
| Zero pieces published in any 7-day window | any | The system failed, not the willpower. Halve production quality bar, batch-film weekly |
| Onsite review rejected | day 75 | Read the stated reason; usually production or hype. Refilm 3, resubmit — approvals on second pass are normal |

## Kill criteria (written now, while sober)

Kill the **niche** — not the whole venture — and restart at Days 1–7 with
the second-ranked niche if, at day 90, with 50+ pieces genuinely published:

- total clicks are still under ~500/month AND trending flat, or
- clicks exist but conversion is near zero (the niche browses, doesn't buy), or
- every earning product is one you can't repeat (one-off viral spike).

Kill the **venture** if you hit day 90 without 50 pieces published. That's
not a market verdict, but it is a verdict: this model needs daily production,
and it's better to know for the cost of 90 days than a year.

**Sunk-cost clause:** hours invested are not a reason to continue. The 50
videos are not wasted either way — production skill and the camera habit
transfer to whatever's next.

## After 90 days — the investor's ledger

What you should own by now, in order of actual value:

1. A production habit and a filter that finds sellable products daily.
2. A library of honest reviews (onsite placements keep paying with zero
   maintenance — that's the closest thing to passive in this business).
3. A channel with an audience in a buying niche.
4. Some commission income — the *smallest* asset on the list.

Item 4 is rented from Amazon and gets repriced whenever Amazon feels like
it (2020, 2026). Items 1–3 are yours, and they are what the next layer is
built on: brand-direct attribution deals (brands pay creators above-rate-card
commissions for Amazon traffic via attribution platforms — only accessible
once you have an audience), sponsorships, and eventually products of your
own. The commissions were never the business. They were the scoreboard while
you built it.
