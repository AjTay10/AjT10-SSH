description: Stress-test a plan — steelman it, attack it five ways, then convert survivors into tripwires and kill criteria.
---

Attack whatever the user describes in $ARGUMENTS (or the plan under discussion).

Work through, in order:

1. **Steelman it first.** State the strongest version of the plan in its
   proponent's terms. If you cannot say why a smart person would do this, your
   critique will miss.

2. **Run the five attacks** from the `red-team` skill: mechanism, adversary,
   base rate, load, and incentive. Skipping one is where the miss happens.

3. **Run a pre-mortem** on whatever survives — assume it failed twelve months
   out and write the causes in past tense.

4. **Convert to tripwires.** Every surviving critical or major finding gets
   an earliest observable signal, a numeric threshold, and a check date.

5. **Write kill criteria.** In numbers, pre-committed, before any money is
   spent.

Rules:
- Cap at three critical findings. More means you are pattern-matching.
- Every critical and major finding needs the cheapest test that would settle
  it, ideally doable today.
- If the plan is genuinely sound, say so plainly and stop. Manufacturing a
  criticism to look rigorous is the failure mode here.
- Say explicitly what you could not evaluate and what you would need to see.
