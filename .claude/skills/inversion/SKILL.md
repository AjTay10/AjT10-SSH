# Inversion

"All I want to know is where I'm going to die, so I'll never go there."

Forward questions have huge, vague answer spaces. *How do I grow an audience?*
has ten thousand answers, all generic. Inverted questions have small, sharp,
concrete ones. *How would I guarantee nobody follows me?* has maybe fifteen —
and every one is specific, testable, and probably something you are already
doing.

## Procedure

**1. State the goal, then invert it into a guarantee.**

Not "how do I avoid failing" — too soft. Use: *"What would I do if my only
job were to make absolutely certain this fails?"* The word *certain* is what
forces specificity.

**2. Generate the sabotage list.** Aim for 12–20 items. Be operational and
petty — the small mechanical things, not the grand strategic ones. Grand
sabotage ("have no strategy") teaches nothing. Petty sabotage ("post at 3am
in my audience's timezone", "open every video with 'hey guys, so today'",
"never reply to a comment") is immediately actionable.

**3. Mark what you are already doing.** This is the payload of the exercise.
Go through the list and honestly tag each: *doing this now*, *sometimes*,
*no*. The items tagged "doing this now" are your highest-leverage fixes, and
you found them without needing a single new idea.

**4. Invert each live item into a concrete stop-doing.** Not "post better" —
"stop opening with a greeting; first frame states the payoff." Stop-doings
ship faster than start-doings because they need no new capacity.

**5. Rank by cost-to-stop.** Free and immediate first.

## Worked example

Goal: grow a YouTube channel.
Inverted: *how do I guarantee the channel never grows?*

```
SABOTAGE                                              DOING IT?   FIX (a stop-doing)
Title the video what it is, not why anyone cares      yes         Title the tension, not the topic
Open with 40s of preamble before the payoff           yes         First 3s states the payoff
Change topic every video so no one knows what I am    sometimes   One topic for the next 12
Never look at retention graphs                        yes         Check the 30s drop-off each upload
Publish whenever I feel like it                       yes         Fixed weekly slot
Copy a format that only works at 10M subs             sometimes   Copy formats from 2x my size
Make thumbnails that need reading to understand       yes         One idea, legible at 120px
Ignore comments                                       yes         Reply to first 20, always
```

Seven fixes, all free, all this week, none of which came from asking "how do
I grow?" — which would have returned "post consistently and make good content".

## When inversion beats forward planning

- The forward answers are all generic → inversion is sharper
- The user is stuck or overwhelmed → stop-doings need no new capacity
- The domain is one where avoiding stupidity beats seeking brilliance:
  investing, health, reputation, hiring, security, anything with tail risk
- Success is rare and failure is common → there is more signal in failure

## When it does not help

Genuinely novel creation with no established failure modes, and situations
where the goal is to maximize upside rather than avoid downside. Inversion
protects; it does not invent.
