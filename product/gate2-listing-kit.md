## What the product is (the wedge, from the research)

A budget-by-paycheck spreadsheet that is everything the flawed
best-sellers are not:

- **Works in Excel AND Google Sheets** — the top sellers (3.5k and 5.3k
  reviews) are Sheets-only and say so in their own titles. Every formula
  in this file is from the universally-compatible set; no import breakage.
- **Undated** — no year branding, never expires, no annual re-buy nag.
- **Four pay frequencies** — weekly, biweekly, semi-monthly, monthly —
  driven by one dropdown. Most competitors hardcode biweekly.
- Auto-assigns every logged expense to the right paycheck by date.
- Editable categories, dashboard with per-paycheck and per-category
  planned-vs-actual, one chart.
